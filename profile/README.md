### Built for the time between two sets

- **Last time is already there.** Weight and reps from your last session are prefilled. If they fit, one tap is enough.
- **Rest starts on its own.** After every set the rest timer runs with your time for that exercise. Need longer? One tap adds 30 seconds.
- **Timers inside your routine.** A Tabata or EMOM finisher sits in your routine as its own block. Afterwards you log what you managed.
- **Plans that fit your week.** Ready-made plans for strength and hybrid training, with progression week by week.
- **Runs in your browser.** Add it to your home screen. Apps for iPhone and Android are on the way.

Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP, For Time and more at **[timer.yabso.app](https://timer.yabso.app)**. No sign-up, works in the browser.
